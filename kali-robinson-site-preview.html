About

I'm an IT consultant and systems analyst who works across the full surface of an organization's technology — from imaging a laptop on a hospital floor to repairing DFS replication across a mixed-version Active Directory forest.

Most recently I was a Tier II NOC Analyst at a managed-services provider: the escalation point for desktop, network, telecom and access issues across multiple client organizations, administering Active Directory, Entra ID and Microsoft 365, hardening email with DMARC/DKIM/SPF, and responding to security incidents with Defender and EDR tooling.

I own tickets end to end, escalate cleanly, and document so the next person doesn't have to rediscover what I already learned. An English degree from Amherst College means I can explain technical risk in plain language — which is most of consulting.
